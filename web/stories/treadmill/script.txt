The treadmill was invented to punish criminals.

England, 1818. Engineer William Cubitt saw prisoners sitting around. So he built a giant wheel with steps.

Prisoners climbed it for hours. Up, up, up. Going nowhere.

Every day, as high as a mountain. Then again tomorrow.

You see, walking uphill is like carrying groceries upstairs. It burns way more than flat walking.

So put your treadmill on an incline today.

Prisoners hated it. Now we pay for it.
