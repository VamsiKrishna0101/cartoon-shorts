Dumbbells were invented to be quiet.

London, 1711. Joseph Addison wanted a bell ringer's workout. But bells are loud.

So he built a bell with no bell. Rope and lead weights. Pull. Silence. Pull. Silence.

A bell that can't ring is a dumb bell. The name stuck.

You see, lowering a weight is like putting down a sleeping baby. Slow builds more muscle.

So lower your dumbbells slowly.

Dumbbells were born silent. Then gym guys started dropping them.
