The burpee was meant to be done four times. Total.

New York, 1939. Scientist Royal Burpee wanted a quick fitness test. Squat. Hands down. Kick back. Stand up.

Four reps, check your heart, done.

Then the army grabbed it. Coaches added a push-up. And a jump. And fifty more.

You see, a burpee is like hot sauce. A little wakes you up. A whole bottle ruins dinner.

So do short sets with clean form.

Mr. Burpee made a test. We made it a punishment.
