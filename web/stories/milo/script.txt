Ancient Greece. A wrestler named Milo of Croton. Every single day, he picked up a newborn calf and carried it.

The calf grew. Milo grew more.

Years later, legend says, he walked into the Olympic stadium with a full-grown bull on his shoulders. The crowd went wild.

Then he ate it.

You see, that's progressive overload. Add a little weight every week, and your body keeps up.
